========== SLIDE 4 ==========
POSITIONING
From digital visibility to intelligent detection
EXISTING ECOSYSTEM
eSAKSHI
MoSPI's national MPLADS workflow and monitoring platform
Data capture across the MPLADS lifecycle
Recommendation & sanction workflow
Fund release and utilisation tracking
Status dashboards for stakeholders
OUR CONTRIBUTION
MPLADS Sentinel
An AI-powered intelligence layer over the existing ecosystem
Detection of anomalies and risk patterns
Correlation across records, documents & entities
Prioritisation of works for human review
Explainable evidence for every risk flag
OVER
eSAKSHI is the system of record and workflow. MPLADS Sentinel is the system of intelligence — it complements eSAKSHI and does not replace it.
